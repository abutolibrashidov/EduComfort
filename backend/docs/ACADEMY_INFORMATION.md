## 🎯 Core Offerings & Programs

### **1. IELTS Preparation**
- **Target Students**: International students preparing for IELTS exam
- **Format**: Mock tests (Reading, Listening, Writing, Speaking)
- **Materials**: 
  - Solutions series textbooks (Beginner, Intermediate)
  - Practice workbooks with gap-filling exercises
  - Past IELTS papers
- **Duration**: Flexible (weeks/months based on student level)
- **Output**: Band scores on 9.0 scale

### **2. General English Courses**
- **Levels**: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced
- **Format**: Online & In-person classes
- **Focus Areas**: 
  - Grammar fundamentals
  - Vocabulary building
  - Speaking confidence
  - Writing skills
- **Textbooks Used**: Solutions series

### **3. Online Courses**
- **Format**: Self-paced, Live, Hybrid
- **Subjects**: English, Math, Subject-specific courses
- **Features**:
  - Video lessons
  - Interactive modules
  - Live class sessions
  - Assignments & projects
  - Progress tracking

### **4. Workbook/Material System**
- **Purpose**: Gap-filling practice exercises
- **Materials**: 
  - PDF textbooks (Solutions series)
  - DOCX worksheets
  - PPT presentations
  - Additional handouts
- **Process**: Teacher uploads → Student completes gaps → Teacher grades
- **Not Auto-graded**: Manual teacher review required

### **5. Exams & Assessments**
- **Regular Exams**: Chapter tests, module tests, final exams
- **Auto-graded**: Objective questions (MCQ, T/F, Matching)
- **Manual Review**: Essays, short answers, writing tasks
- **IELTS Mock Tests**: Full practice exams with band scoring

## 📊 Academic Structure

### **IELTS Preparation Path**
```
Diagnostic Test → Identify Level → Enroll in Appropriate Course
                           ↓
        Attend live classes & online materials
                           ↓
        Complete practice workbooks & assignments
                           ↓
        Take IELTS mock tests (Reading/Listening/Writing)
                           ↓
        Get band scores & feedback
                           ↓
        Identify weak areas & retake if needed
                           ↓
```

## 📊 Performance & Grading

### **Grading Scale**
- **A+**: 90-100% (Excellent)
- **A**: 85-89% (Very Good)
- **B+**: 80-84% (Good)
- **B**: 75-79% (Satisfactory)
- **C**: 70-74% (Average)
- **D**: 60-69% (Below Average)
- **F**: <60% (Fail)

### **IELTS Band Scale**
- Band 9: Native speaker level
- Band 8: Very good user
- Band 7: Good user
- Band 6: Competent user
- Band 5: Modest user
- Band 4: Limited user
- Band 3: Extremely limited user
- Band 1-2: Non-user
